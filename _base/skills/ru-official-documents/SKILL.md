# Russian Official Documents

## Core Goal

Produce paperwork that a Russian university official can sign without rewriting it. The genre is narrow and formulaic on purpose: a recommendation letter is not an essay, and originality in it reads as a defect.

## The Sample Outranks Taste

When the user supplies an образец, reproduce its structure and register instead of writing something "better". Mine the sample for:

- presence or absence of an addressee block (many accepted samples have none — no «В конкурсную комиссию»);
- the heading wording and centring;
- the order of paragraphs;
- the closing formula, copied nearly verbatim (`С учётом вышеизложенного, рекомендую рассмотреть кандидатуру … для участия в конкурсе на замещение должности … в …`);
- the signature line: должность, инициалы перед фамилией, one line;
- whether a date and a «"__" ______ 20__ г.» block exist at all — if the sample has none, do not add them.

A scan is usually the only form of the sample. Render it to images and read it visually; extracted text loses the layout, and the layout is half the convention.

## No Invented Observation

Never write a personal observation the signatory did not make. Sentences like «занятия ведёт спокойно и по существу», «объясняет не формулу саму по себе, а её происхождение», «умение усомниться в готовом решении встречается реже, чем аккуратность» are the classic failure: they sound like literary character sketching, they are unverifiable, and they mark the text as machine-written. The user rejected exactly these (25.08.2026).

Two sources are allowed instead:

1. **Facts backed by a document** — dates, оценки, названия докладов, приказы, объём программы, решения кафедры.
2. **The stock evaluative formulas of the genre**, which are expected and carry no claim of personal insight: «имеет неконфликтный характер», «отличается высоким уровнем ответственности и трудолюбия», «в коллективе пользуется уважением и авторитетом», «проявил ответственность и добросовестность», «зарекомендовал себя дисциплинированным и инициативным».

When a characterisation is genuinely needed, lift the wording from an existing signed отзыв — отчёт по педагогической практике, аттестация аспиранта, отзыв руководителя ВКР. «Справился с задачами, приобрёл необходимые навыки и компетенции» is a real sentence from a real form; an invented equivalent is not.

## Nothing That Reads As A Claim Against A Third Party

Cut anything that can be read as criticism of a colleague, even when factually true and flattering to the subject: found an error in someone's program, corrected a predecessor's method, outperformed a group. These documents circulate inside a small collective, and the person implicitly criticised often sits on the same кафедра, co-authors the same papers, or votes on the same совет. The user cut such a passage himself (25.08.2026): it named a defect in code written by a senior colleague and co-author.

Keep the underlying strength by restating it as a property of the subject's own work — verification against an independent calculation, agreement with numerical modelling, reproducibility — without naming what was wrong or whose it was.

## Several Letters In One Package

A конкурс often needs two recommendations, e.g. from руководитель структурного подразделения and научный руководитель. Split them by role so they do not read as copies:

- научный руководитель — тема, результаты, публикации, аттестация, научная самостоятельность;
- руководитель подразделения — преподавание, методические материалы, работа со студентами, поведение в коллективе, трудовая деятельность.

The overlapping facts (education, degree with honours, конференции) may repeat; the emphasis must not.

## Facts Discipline

- State a year, номер приказа, оценка, or объём only when a document in the vault confirms it. If the year of a conference cannot be confirmed, name the conference without a year rather than guessing.
- Prefer the official wording of a теме, программы, or должности over a working paraphrase; drafts in the vault often carry an outdated variant.
- Do not inflate: «около четырёх лет» backed by an СФР statement beats «более полутора лет» taken from an incomplete трудовая книжка, and both beat an unsourced round number.

## Drafting For Someone Else's Signature

The draft exists so the signatory reviews and corrects rather than composes. Keep it to one page, keep every claim checkable, and hand the file over with a short note on which facts they should verify before signing. Store the draft next to the конкурс materials in the vault, not in a scratch folder.
